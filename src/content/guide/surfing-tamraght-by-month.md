## The short version

Tamraght is the village on Morocco's Atlantic coast where the three houses we work with are, and this is what surfing there looks like from October to April, month by month. Tamraght and Taghazout are about 6 to 8 km apart on the same stretch of coast, so the Agadir figures below are a close guide for both. For the village itself, including year-round weather, see the [Tamraght village guide](/guide/tamraght/).

When is the best time to surf near Tamraght? Roughly October to April, when north-west Atlantic swells arrive most often, with the biggest swells usually from December to March; beginners can also learn in the smaller summer waves. This follows [The Surf Atlas](https://thesurfatlas.com/surf-travel/morocco-surf-season/) (updated 2026-04-20), which calls December to March the peak and June to August the low season. The two Surf Atlas guides define the low season slightly differently (June to August in one, April to September in the other), so treat the edges of the season as approximate.

Over winter the sea off Agadir averages about 17.5 to 18.3 °C from December to April. [O'Neill's wetsuit chart](https://eu.oneill.com/pages/wetsuit-season-guide) suggests a 3/2 mm or 4/3 mm suit for 18 to 20 °C water and a 4/3 mm or 5/4 mm suit for 12 to 17 °C, and calls the chart a guideline.

### How warm is the sea in Agadir in winter?

The sea off Agadir averages about 17.5 to 18.3 °C from December to April, and February is the coldest month in that table at about 17.5 °C ([Weather Atlas, Agadir climate](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/morocco/agadir-climate), checked 2026-11-06). Other sites differ by about a degree: [seatemperature.info](https://www.seatemperature.info/agadir-water-temperature.html), which says it averages the past 10 years of satellite and in-situ data, lists 17.2 °C for January and 16.6 °C for February.

### What wetsuit do you need to surf in Morocco in winter?

A 4/3 mm suit is the middle choice for most people in Agadir's winter sea of roughly 17 to 18 °C. [O'Neill's wetsuit chart](https://eu.oneill.com/pages/wetsuit-season-guide) gives these bands: 3/2 mm or 4/3 mm for 18 to 20 °C, 4/3 mm or 5/4 mm for 12 to 17 °C, and a 2 mm suit or shorty for 21 to 25 °C. It says the chart is only a guideline and that other factors play a role. Applied to Agadir's winter sea of roughly 17 to 18 °C, that points to a 3/2 mm to 5/4 mm suit with 4/3 mm in the middle, and a thicker or thinner choice depends on how cold you run. Whether a wetsuit comes with your stay is something we'll confirm with you at booking, so ask us before you decide what to pack.

## Surfing Tamraght in October

In October the sea off Agadir averages about 20.7 °C, which sits between O'Neill's 2 mm and 3/2 mm bands. According to [The Surf Atlas](https://thesurfatlas.com/surf-travel/morocco-surf-season/), north-west swells pick up and the famous points switch back on, with less than full winter intensity, and Tamraght is the main beginner beach. (Weather Atlas, checked 2026-11-06.)

October is the start of the season rather than the middle of it. Air temperatures average about 15 °C at night to 25 °C in the day.

To see which spots suit a beginner in a given swell, read [Tamraght's surf spots by level and tide](/guide/surfing-tamraght/).

## Surfing Tamraght in November

In November the sea off Agadir averages about 19.4 °C, which is in O'Neill's 3/2 mm or 4/3 mm band. [The Surf Atlas](https://thesurfatlas.com/surf-travel/morocco-surf-season/) describes November swell as bigger and more regular, with conditions around Tamraght workable for improvers and the points getting more demanding. (Weather Atlas, checked 2026-11-06.)

### Is November a good month to learn to surf in Tamraght?

Beginners can learn in November: the water is still mild and The Surf Atlas describes conditions around Tamraght as workable for improvers. Conditions change from day to day with the swell and tide, so a flexible plan works better than a fixed one.

Air temperatures average about 12 °C at night to 24 °C in the day. The [spot guide](/guide/surfing-tamraght/) explains which beaches get used when the swell picks up.

## Surfing Tamraght in December

In December the sea off Agadir averages about 18.3 °C (3/2 mm or 4/3 mm on O'Neill's chart), and December to March is the peak swell season according to [The Surf Atlas](https://thesurfatlas.com/surf-travel/morocco-surf-season/). For beginners that means some days are ideal and some are for watching, so a flexible week suits it better than a fixed plan. (Weather Atlas, checked 2026-11-06.)

In winter the famous point breaks further up the coast work best. [The Surf Atlas's Taghazout guide](https://thesurfatlas.com/surfing-morocco/taghazout-surf/) rates Anchor Point intermediate and up, Killer Point expert, and Mysteries and Panorama upper intermediate or intermediate and up. Our [Taghazout points guide](/guide/surfing-taghazout/) explains why you can skip them for now. Air temperatures average about 9 °C at night to 21 °C in the day.

If you're travelling over Christmas and New Year, book early and ask us how availability looks for your dates.

## Surfing Tamraght in January

In January the sea off Agadir averages about 18.3 °C (3/2 mm or 4/3 mm on O'Neill's chart), in the middle of the December to March peak swell season. For beginners that means learning is possible, but expect some days to be too big for a first-timer; Tamraght's beaches are the usual beginner choice while the points suit more experienced surfers. (Weather Atlas, checked 2026-11-06; [The Surf Atlas](https://thesurfatlas.com/surf-travel/morocco-surf-season/).)

January is among the cooler months at sea. The Weather Atlas table has December at the same 18.3 °C and February lower at 17.5 °C, while seatemperature.info puts January at 17.2 °C. Air temperatures average about 8 °C at night to 20 °C in the day, so mornings and evenings feel cooler than the afternoon.

Experienced surfers who want the bigger waves should read the [Taghazout points guide](/guide/surfing-taghazout/) and the [Tamraght spot guide](/guide/surfing-tamraght/) together.

## Surfing Tamraght in February

In February the sea off Agadir averages about 17.5 °C, the lowest of the seven months in the Weather Atlas table; 4/3 mm is the one thickness listed in both of O'Neill's neighbouring bands. Swells are still in the December to March peak, so the approach is the same as January. (Weather Atlas, checked 2026-11-06.)

Air temperatures average about 9 °C at night to 21 °C in the day. February can still be cold at sea, so don't pack as if spring has arrived.

## Surfing Tamraght in March

In March the sea off Agadir averages about 17.7 °C (4/3 mm on O'Neill's chart), and March is still within the December to March peak swell period, so expect the same pattern as winter: Tamraght's beaches for beginners, the points for experienced surfers. (Weather Atlas, checked 2026-11-06; [The Surf Atlas](https://thesurfatlas.com/surf-travel/morocco-surf-season/).)

Air temperatures average about 11 °C at night to 22 °C in the day.

## Surfing Tamraght in April

In April the sea off Agadir averages about 18.0 °C (3/2 mm or 4/3 mm on O'Neill's chart). [The Surf Atlas](https://thesurfatlas.com/surf-travel/morocco-surf-season/) describes April as leftover Atlantic swell in more manageable sizes with fewer surfers as Europeans head home, and suggests it suits intermediates best, though it is fun around Taghazout. (Weather Atlas, checked 2026-11-06.)

Air temperatures average about 12 °C at night to 22 °C in the day.

## And the summer?

Summer waves around Tamraght are usually smaller and less consistent than in winter. [The Surf Atlas](https://thesurfatlas.com/surf-travel/morocco-surf-season/) describes June to August as the low season, mostly windswell, suited to beginners and improvers, with strong winds a recurring issue and worst in summer. The sea off Agadir averages about 19.7 °C in June, 20.2 °C in July and 20.8 °C in August (Weather Atlas, checked 2026-11-06), and you can still learn then.

Summer means fewer big days and more flat ones. If regular waves matter more to you than a quiet line-up, winter and early spring are the better fit.
